Privacy Policy — nextBus

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Data We Process

nextBus provides real-time public transit arrival estimates in Israel via Google Connected Apps and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), as well as our standalone web widget. When you request bus status or route directions, your query transmits your requested line number or destination and approximate GPS coordinates solely to locate nearby public transit stops and public transit routes.

2. Ephemeral Caching, Anonymous Route History & No Personal Tracking

Live bus telemetry is cached transiently in Redis for up to 60 seconds and static route pairs up to 3 hours. In standalone web/mobile mode, the app generates a random anonymous device identifier (nextbus_user_id stored in browser localStorage) strictly to remember your recent transit searches (user_route_history) near your departure area (~250 m radius) so you can re-select frequent routes with one tap.

We do not collect personally identifiable information (no names, emails, phone numbers, or accounts), we do not perform cross-site advertising tracking, and we never sell or share user data with advertisers. You may reset your anonymous identifier and history at any time by clearing your browser storage.

3. Third-Party Data Sources

Real-time vehicle telemetry is retrieved asynchronously by background workers from the Open Bus Stride API, an open-data service operated by The Public Knowledge Workshop (Hasadna), which republishes official government open data. No personal data is sent to these sources.

4. Government Data Sources & Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

nextBus is an independent application. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Israeli Ministry of Transport and Road Safety, the National Public Transport Authority, or any other government entity.

Public transit schedule (GTFS) and real-time (SIRI) information displayed in nextBus is derived from publicly available open data published by the National Public Transport Authority (Israel Ministry of Transport and Road Safety):

  • National Public Transport Authority (Israel Ministry of Transport)
  • Official GTFS Static Schedule Data
  • Official SIRI Real-Time Data
  • Open Data Aggregator — The Public Knowledge Workshop (Open Bus Stride API)
