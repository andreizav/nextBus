Terms of Service — nextBus

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Service Scope

nextBus provides informational real-time arrival estimates for Israeli public transit routes and optional nearby venue recommendations.

2. Accuracy Disclaimer

Estimated Times of Arrival (ETA) and vehicle locations depend on upstream SIRI and GTFS telemetry feeds from public transit operators and the Open Bus Stride API. Estimates are provided "as is" without warranty of exact arrival times.

3. Fair Use

Automated scraping or abusive high-frequency polling bypassing the official MCP and widget endpoints is prohibited to protect upstream open-data rate limits.
